About me

I'm based in Tirana, with a BSc and an MSc in Telecommunication Engineering from the Polytechnic University of Tirana. Networks and signals taught me to think in systems: how data moves, where it breaks, and how to prove it works.

Today I'm a technical recruiter at IMPAX, screening software and AI engineers for US startups in San Francisco and New York. Reading engineering profiles every day keeps me close to how modern teams build. My next step is a software engineering role in Albania.
